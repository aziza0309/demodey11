Frequently Asked Questions

Do you deliver to all areas?
Currently, our lightning-fast 30-minute delivery is available within a 15km radius of our branches. We are actively expanding to cover more zones!
Is there a pickup discount?
Yes! If you select "Pickup" as your delivery method, an automatic 10% discount is applied to your entire cart. Plus, there's no waiting in line.
Can I book a table for a large event?
Absolutely. For groups larger than 8 people, please use the Reservation form on our Services page or contact us directly. We offer custom menus and special birthday setups.
How do the secret promo codes work?
We hide Easter Eggs across our website. If you find a code, enter it in the Secret Vault on the Services page to unlock free items or massive discounts instantly.
